03 / Engineering Journey

A documented timeline of engineering work, team milestones, and projects in development.

  1. August 2025
    01

    Getting Started in Rocketry

    Art Learning Mission (Team 2)—the team's official name—formed on August 19, 2025. Its first team rocket launch attempt followed on August 24, 2025; this milestone does not establish a successful recovery.

  2. December 2025 – May 2026
    02

    Rocket Flight Solver

    Developed a Python-based tool for rocket apogee estimation and liftoff-mass optimization. GitHub commit history confirms project activity from December 2025 through May 2026. The tool has not been independently validated against real-flight accuracy.

    View repository (opens in a new tab)
  3. May 2026
    03

    American Rocketry Challenge National Finals

    Art Learning Mission (Team 2) placed 42nd at the 2026 National Finals. This is the team's placement, not an individual ranking. I served as Team Captain, contributing to rocket design, team coordination, and project documentation.

    View official results (opens in a new tab)
  4. October 2, 2026
    04

    V4 Flight Computer — Rev A Design Snapshot

    Advanced a six-layer RP2040-based flight-computer design through Rev A manufacturing-package preparation and consistency review.

    In development · Hardware design

    No claim is made that the PCB was manufactured, physically tested, or flown.

  5. October 2026
    05

    Thunder V2 — HPR Flight Experience

    Participated in structural design, fabrication and assembly, motor and recovery configuration, launch operations, and post-flight data review. AltimeterTwo original display video shows a peak-altitude reading of 2,329 ft.

    The exact launch date remains unconfirmed. No claim is made about OpenRocket preflight-prediction accuracy or a complete recovery sequence.
