07 / Reflection

Engineering Challenges & Lessons Learned

This evidence review highlights documentation practices that make a future flight narrative more technically useful without turning gaps or recollections into completed engineering results.

  • Capture the as-flown state. A model or planned configuration cannot replace launch-day mass, setup, and hardware records.
  • Keep source labels attached. Motor, delay, parachute, JLCR, and altitude details remain user-reported until original evidence is reviewed.
  • A peak value is not a flight profile. One altitude reading cannot establish ascent, ejection, descent, or recovery dynamics.
  • Compare only approved baselines. Simulation accuracy requires a traceable preflight prediction and a reviewed flight measurement from the same configuration.
